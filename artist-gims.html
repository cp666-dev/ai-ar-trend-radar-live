Bio · Wikipedia
Gandhi Alimasi Djuna, known professionally as Maître Gims and, since around 2019, just Gims, is a Congolese singer and rapper. He grew up in France and currently lives in France and Morocco. Gims rose to fame as a member of the hip hop group Sexion d'Assaut and pursued an individual career under the name Maître Gims in 2013. In 2006, he released an extended play, titled Ceux qui dorment les yeux ouverts.

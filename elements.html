How this works

Every track's audio fingerprint is compared against 87 production element descriptions through the same audio-language model that powers the sounds. A track is tagged only when the model hears an element distinctly more than the rest of the corpus (z ≥ 1.6), so tags mean "this stands out here", not "this might be present".
A solo-built, self-running product: multi-source ingestion, CLAP audio embeddings, persistent sound families, baseline-relative velocity, Claude analyst notes. Rebuilt daily by an unattended pipeline. Clustering validated by ear: 78% genre purity. Previews stream from their source platforms and belong to their rights holders.

RADAR Sounds Artists Track record Method RUN 2026-09-14

Charts say what won.
Audio says what's winning.

Chart trackers work top-down from positions, after the fact. Radar works bottom-up from the audio: a production style quietly accumulating tracks shows up here before any chart moves.

Six steps, daily

1 · Ingest~1,500 track appearances from Deezer charts and editorial playlists, Apple's most-played feed, and Reddit. Every appearance is stored with source, rank, day.
2 · ListenEach 30s preview becomes an audio fingerprint via CLAP, a model that hears production, instrumentation and mood. Tempo, key and energy come along.
3 · GroupFingerprints are clustered, then matched to persistent sound families so "this sound" keeps its identity day to day.
4 · NameFamilies are named by comparing their fingerprint against hundreds of genre and mood phrases. The names describe what the model hears.
5 · MeasureEach family's share of the day's intake is tracked against its 7-day baseline. Growth ≥40% at z ≥ 2 is a flag; raw counts alone never are.
6 · ExplainClaude writes a 3-sentence note for the top sounds from the measured stats only, so it cannot invent viral moments.

Verified by ear

Before anything was built on the embeddings, clustering was tested on 100 tracks from five distinct genres: 78% purity, metal 20/20. Listen and judge. Most "errors" are the model refusing to believe Deezer's classical chart, which contains Pitbull. The model is right.

Under the hood

Python · CLAP + librosa · scikit-learn · SQLite · Claude API · zero servers. A GitHub Actions cron runs everything daily and publishes this static site. Playback fetches fresh preview URLs at click time because stored ones expire within hours.
A solo-built, self-running product: multi-source ingestion, CLAP audio embeddings, persistent sound families, baseline-relative velocity, Claude analyst notes. Rebuilt daily by an unattended pipeline. Clustering validated by ear: 78% genre purity. Previews stream from their source platforms and belong to their rights holders.

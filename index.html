A&R TREND RADAR

Every day this radar listens to ~1,500 new and trending tracks and groups them by how they actually sound — not by genre tags or chart position. When one sound starts pulling in tracks faster than usual, that's an early trend signal, often weeks before it reaches the charts.

1 · ListenTracks are pulled daily from Deezer charts & editorial playlists, iTunes, and Reddit music communities. Each 30-second preview is analyzed by an AI audio model (CLAP) that hears production, instrumentation and mood.
2 · Group by soundTracks that sound alike cluster together automatically. Each persistent sound gets a name from the audio itself — the same AI model matches the cluster's sonic fingerprint against hundreds of genre and mood descriptions.
3 · Watch the growthEach sound's share of the daily intake is tracked against its 7-day baseline. Unusual growth (statistically, not just raw counts) flags a trend — and every call is kept on record and scored against what later charts.

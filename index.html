Analyst note · Claude · 2026-10-01

This cluster sits in melodic rap territory with a hard-hitting, bass heavy low end, which lines up with the top audio tags (melodic rap at 0.515 and hard-hitting and bass heavy at 0.411). Mean energy is moderate at 0.54, so these are not wall-to-wall bangers but tracks with weight and space for vocal melody. The tempo range is wide, from about 83 to 162 BPM, which suggests the cluster spans slower, more sung cuts and faster, harder ones rather than one single tempo lane. Keys lean toward G major, A minor, and D minor, a mix of bright and darker tonal centers. You can hear the family in tracks like "Backwards" by Quavo, "The Team" by Central Cee, and "MISS MY DAWG" by Yeat, which anchor the melodic rap and bass heavy feel. Note that dance pop (0.487) and boom bap (0.458) also score high, so the edges of this cluster are a little blurry and it may pull in adjacent sounds.

I cannot call this a trend yet. The velocity status is baseline with only 3 days of history, and both growth and z-score are null, so there is no measured momentum to point to. That said, the release dates are genuinely recent, with several tracks from mid to late September 2026 and one, "Histoire sans fin" by BEN plg, dated October 2, 2026, which tells us the cluster is being fed by fresh material rather than catalog. To call this moving, I would need more days of history past the baseline window plus a positive growth percentage and a z-score that clears the noise, showing today's 15 tracks is a real climb and not just a starting count. Until then, treat this as one to watch, not one to sign off on.

How a call is made

charts say what won; audio says what is winning, six steps run daily, unattended python · clap · scikit-learn · sqlite · zero servers
01ingest

~1,500 track appearances from Deezer charts, editorial playlists, Apple's most-played feed and Reddit. Only releases from the last 60 days go on the radar.

02listen

Each 30-second preview becomes an audio fingerprint via CLAP, a model that hears production, instrumentation and mood. Tempo, key and energy come along for free.

03group

Fingerprints are clustered, then matched to persistent sound families so a sound keeps its identity from one day to the next.

04name

Families are named by comparing their fingerprint against hundreds of genre and mood phrases. Names describe what the model hears, not what a label claims.

05measure

Each family's share of the day's intake is tracked against its 7-day baseline. Growth ≥ 40% at z ≥ 2 is a flag. Raw counts alone never are.

06explain

Claude writes an analyst note for the top sounds from the measured statistics only, so it cannot invent a viral moment that did not happen.

Verified by ear

78% genre purity, metal 20/20

Before anything was built on the embeddings, clustering was tested by hand on 100 tracks across five distinct genres. Most remaining "errors" are the model refusing to believe Deezer's classical chart, which contains Pitbull. The model is right.

Under the hood

One cron, no servers

A GitHub Actions job runs ingestion, embedding, clustering and publishing every morning and ships this static site. Playback fetches fresh preview URLs at click time because stored ones expire within hours.

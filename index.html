This cluster lands under melodic rap as its strongest audio tag at 0.481, but the scores sit close together, with country at 0.466 and dance pop at 0.465 right behind, so the sound is melodic and vocal-forward rather than locked to one lane. Energy is middle of the road at 0.50, the tempo stretches wide from about 78 to 136 BPM, and the keys skew minor with F minor, F# minor, and E minor common, which tracks with a mood that reads euphoric but still soft-edged. You can hear the through-line in Didi B's "JURU," Tiakola's "Jet Lag," and Victony's "SLICK (Remix)," which share the sung-rap delivery and uplifting melodic hooks that define the group. Note the real spread here, though, with Warren Zeiders' "Best Shot" pulling toward country and Judeline's "La Falda" toward a more pop-leaning sound, so this is a loose family, not a tight single style.

I can call this one moving, with the usual caution. Velocity is measured over 8 days of history, which clears the three-day minimum needed to read a trend rather than noise, and it shows 47.4 percent growth with a z of 3.65, meaning the recent lift is well above this cluster's own baseline. Release recency backs that up, since most of the representative tracks dropped in October and November 2026, with several from early to mid November, so fresh material is feeding the rise rather than old catalog resurfacing. The honest caveat is that 8 days is still a short window and the tag spread is wide, so I would want another week of history to confirm the growth holds and to see whether the melodic rap core or the country and dance pop edges are driving it.

How a call is made

charts say what won; audio says what is winning, six steps run daily, unattended python · clap · scikit-learn · sqlite · zero servers
01ingest

~1,500 track appearances from Deezer charts, editorial playlists, Apple's most-played feed and Reddit. Only releases from the last 60 days go on the radar.

02listen

Each 30-second preview becomes an audio fingerprint via CLAP, a model that hears production, instrumentation and mood. Tempo, key and energy come along for free.

03group

Fingerprints are clustered, then matched to persistent sound families so a sound keeps its identity from one day to the next.

04name

Families are named by comparing their fingerprint against hundreds of genre and mood phrases. Names describe what the model hears, not what a label claims.

05measure

Each family's share of the day's intake is tracked against its 7-day baseline. Growth ≥ 40% at z ≥ 2 is a flag. Raw counts alone never are.

06explain

Claude writes an analyst note for the top sounds from the measured statistics only, so it cannot invent a viral moment that did not happen.

Verified by ear

78% genre purity, metal 20/20

Before anything was built on the embeddings, clustering was tested by hand on 100 tracks across five distinct genres. Most remaining "errors" are the model refusing to believe Deezer's classical chart, which contains Pitbull. The model is right.

Under the hood

One cron, no servers

A GitHub Actions job runs ingestion, embedding, clustering and publishing every morning and ships this static site. Playback fetches fresh preview URLs at click time because stored ones expire within hours.

RADA/R

A solo-built, self-running product: multi-source ingestion, CLAP audio embeddings, persistent sound families, baseline-relative velocity and Claude analyst notes, rebuilt daily by an unattended pipeline.

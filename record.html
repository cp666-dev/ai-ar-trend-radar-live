Every call, on the record.

When a sound grows at least 40% above its 7-day baseline with statistical significance (z ≥ 2), the radar flags it — timestamped, immutable. From then on, every daily run checks whether tracks from that sound later reach a chart top-20. No cherry-picking: misses stay on the board next to hits.

Why the board is empty
The radar went live on 2026-09-14 and refuses to flag anything until a sound has at least 3 days of measured baseline. The first calls become possible on 2026-09-17. A trend detector that makes calls without a baseline is a random-number generator with good marketing — the empty board is the feature.

How calls are scored

FlagA sound's share of the daily intake exceeds its 7-day baseline by ≥40% with z ≥ 2. The call is committed to the database with its date and stats.
WatchEvery subsequent daily run checks the flagged sound's member tracks against all chart top-20 positions observed that day.
ScoreEach later chart appearance is recorded as evidence with its date — building a verifiable "flagged N days before it charted" record.

How a call is scored

committed, watched, then judged in public no retroactive edits
01flag

Growth ≥ 40% over the 7-day baseline at z ≥ 2. The flag is committed with its date, share, baseline and z-score, permanently.

02watch

Every later run checks the flagged sound's tracks against that day's chart top-20s, for 60 days after the flag.

03score

Each hit is recorded as dated evidence, "flagged N days before it charted." Sounds that never chart are recorded just as visibly.

Bio · Wikipedia · public record

Julia Jacklin is an Australian singer-songwriter from the Blue Mountains region of New South Wales. Jacklin's musical style has been described as indie pop, indie folk, and alternative country. She has released four studio albums, Don't Let the Kids Win (2016), Crushing (2019), Pre Pleasure (2022) and The Gem (2026). Jacklin has also performed with the band Phantastic Ferniture, with whom she released the debut single "Fuckin 'n' Rollin" and a self-titled album in 2018, followed by subsequent singles.
